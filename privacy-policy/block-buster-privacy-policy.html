Block Buster Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Summary

Block Buster does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. The Game works fully offline, has no accounts or logins, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools.

Introduction

Pixel Perfekt Inc ("we", "us") develops and publishes Block Buster (the "Game") for Android. This policy explains how the Game handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information from or about you. The Game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any other personal details, and it does not use your device's advertising ID.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your high score locally on your device so it is there the next time you play. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the Game's data in Settings > Apps > Block Buster > Storage > Clear data, or by uninstalling the Game.

Third-party services

The Game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or sign-in services.

The Game is distributed through Google Play. When you download it, Google may collect information under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive any of that information.

Children's privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not collect information from children.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your locally saved high score is fully under your control, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game adds features that collect information, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before that version is released.
